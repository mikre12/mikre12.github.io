// 02 · research

Where security meets language

Phishing does not arrive in clean English. My research looks at detecting it where most tools struggle, and at making the decision explainable.

Research direction In development

Explainable phishing detection for Amharic–English messaging

Phishing and smishing messages in Ethiopia often mix Amharic and English, a setting most detectors were not built for. I am working on a transformer-based detector (mBERT and XLM-RoBERTa) that also explains why a message was flagged using SHAP and LIME, so users and analysts can trust and act on the decision.

  1. 01Collect & label mixed-language messages
  2. 02Fine-tune mBERT / XLM-RoBERTa
  3. 03Classify: phishing or legitimate
  4. 04Explain with SHAP & LIME
  • Low-resource NLP
  • Explainable AI
  • Smishing
  • Transformers
  • Mobile security
